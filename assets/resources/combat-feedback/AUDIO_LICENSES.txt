rifle-reload.wav combines:
1. "gun bolt.wav" by fennelliott (public HQ MP3 preview).
   https://freesound.org/people/fennelliott/sounds/347360/
   CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
2. "Gun Reload Sound Effects", clipload1.wav and clipload2.wav,
   by BMacZero / Brian MacIntosh.
   https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sound-effects
   CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Changes: selected action fragments, mono conversion, filtering, fades,
gain adjustment, and sequencing into a 3.2-second game reload sound.

These are game Foley effects using related bolt-action rifle recordings,
not recordings of an actual Hanyang rifle or Gewehr 1888. No endorsement
by the original creators is implied. The project's MIT license does not
replace the licenses of these audio recordings. Keep this notice when
redistributing the rifle audio. Full provenance: docs/audio-credits.md

The knife-swing.wav and knife-heavy-swing.wav files are project-original
synthetic effects under the project's MIT license; they are unchanged here.
